I, a user of the V-Tempe mobile app (the "App"), by ticking the box "I consent to the processing
of my health data and to its transfer abroad…" or tapping "I agree", freely, of my own will and
in my own interest give specific, informed, conscious and unambiguous consent under Art. 9,
Art. 10(2)(1) and Art. 12 of Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data" on the following
terms.

## 1. Operator

[OPERATOR NAME], tax ID / registration number [INN/OGRN], address: [ADDRESS], e-mail:
[CONTACT E-MAIL].

## 2. Purpose

Creating and adjusting personal workout, nutrition and sleep plans by the AI coach and its chat
replies taking my health into account, so that exercises and foods dangerous to me are excluded;
keeping a server backup of this data to restore it on another device (if I am signed in).

## 3. Data

- injuries and limitations;
- health notes and contraindications;
- allergies and food intolerances;
- height, weight, weight history, age, sex, goal, experience and daily activity level — insofar
  as, together with the above, they describe my physical condition;
- workout log: completed sets, weights, reps, perceived effort (RPE), notes;
- sleep log: dates, duration, notes;
- health information I write in messages to the AI coach.

## 4. Operations

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use,
transfer (including cross-border), deletion and destruction, by automated means.

My name, e-mail, photo, account ID and IP address are not sent to the AI.

## 6. Term

The consent is valid until withdrawn. Data on my device is kept until I delete it (data reset,
uninstall). The server backup is kept until the account is deleted. Retention at OpenRouter and
model developers: [STATE].

## 7. Withdrawal

I may withdraw consent at any time:

- in the App: Settings → "Legal and consents" → switch the consent off;
- by e-mail to [CONTACT E-MAIL].

After withdrawal the App immediately stops sending my data to the AI coach and AI features are
turned off. The operator stops processing and destroys the data within 30 days unless the law
provides otherwise. Server data can be deleted immediately via Settings → "Delete account".

I understand that without consent AI coach features are unavailable, while the other features
(logs, offline plans) keep working.
